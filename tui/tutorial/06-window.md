# The window: fonts, zoom, and help

Under `autodoc --gui`, the window draws in fonts you choose. **Options › Fonts and zoom…**:

- **the cell font**, the monospace the page, the panels and the menus are drawn in, and its size;
- **the prose font**, of the Rendered view and the HTML preview;
- **the zoom**, 100% to 200%, over all of it. **Ctrl+=** and **Ctrl+-** step it, **Ctrl+0** resets it, and the right-click menu has it too.

In a terminal, the terminal's own settings choose the font and the size.

**Options › Editor preferences…** (SPC ,) sets the rest: the page's width, the theme, wrapping, line numbers, where each panel opens, the notifications' corner.

**Help**: **F1** lists every key and what each needs; **SPC h** the notifications' history; this tutorial is **Help › Tutorial…** (SPC T).
