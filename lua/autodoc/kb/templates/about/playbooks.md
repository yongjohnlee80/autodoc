# playbooks/

- **Holds:** procedures: a release, a diagnosis, a recovery. Numbered steps, with the commands.
- **Naming:** `<kebab-task>.md`.
- **Type:** `playbook`. `status`: draft, active or retired. Bump `revision:` when the steps change.
- **Rules:** a playbook says what each step needs and how to tell it worked.
