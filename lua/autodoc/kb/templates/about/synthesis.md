# synthesis/

- **Holds:** what was learned: analyses, investigations, lessons, bug-fix records, benchmarks, incidents, design notes, evidence, handoffs, research.
- **Naming:** `<kebab-subject>.md`, or `<YYYY-MM-DD>-<kebab-subject>.md` for a dated record.
- **Type:** `synthesis`, with `kind`: analysis, investigation, lesson, bug-fix, benchmark, incident, design, evidence, handoff or research. `status`: draft, final or superseded.
- **Rules:** evidence-anchored: each conclusion points at its source (a file and line, a commit, a measurement).
