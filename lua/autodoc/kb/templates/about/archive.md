# archive/

- **Holds:** retired material: superseded index and log pages, old rules, retired documents.
- **Naming:** files keep their names; a folder mirrors where they came from.
- **Type:** whatever they carried when they were retired.
- **Rules:** frozen. Don't edit archived files or fix their links: they record the layout they were written in. To revive something, copy it out to its folder and edit the copy.
