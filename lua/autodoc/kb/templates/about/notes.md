# notes/

- **Holds:** work in progress: task notes, scratch, incoming material, handoffs, drafts.
- **Naming:** `<YYYY-MM-DD>-<kebab-subject>.md`. Subfolders are allowed.
- **Type:** `note`, with `kind`: task, scratch, incoming, handoff or draft. `status`: active or done. `author:` says whose note it is.
- **Rules:** when a note becomes durable knowledge, move what lasts into the folder it belongs in and mark the note `done`.
